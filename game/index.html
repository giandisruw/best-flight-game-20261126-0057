SKYLINE RING RUSH

An original offline flight game — take off from the valley runway, thread all eight glowing rings over the lake and the mountains, and bring her home in one piece.

W / ↑ climb   S / ↓ descend
A / D or ← / → bank & turn
Mouse steer (move to bank & pitch)   Wheel throttle
Shift / Ctrl throttle up / down   Space airbrake
C cockpit / chase camera   P pause   R restart

100% offline — no network, no assets, no accounts. Runs from static files.
